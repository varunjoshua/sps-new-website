Overview

Heavy Duty Airless Spray System for ESPON Coatings

The ASP-70 is a heavy duty airless spray system designed for tough working conditions. It is suitable for high viscosity, solventless and low solvent coatings, semisolid coatings, and marine coatings.

The system features a pneumatically sensed high-performance air motor with a stainless steel rust-proof hydraulic body, delivering high pressure with high output discharge. Ice-free air motor technology ensures reliable operation, and the unit complies with EC and ATEX norms.
