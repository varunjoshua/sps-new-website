Overview

Effective Die Lubricant Spraying System

The Diaphragm Pump Spray System is a 200-litre die lubricant spraying unit designed to keep water-based graphite lubricant in continuous suspension, ensuring a homogeneous mixture of water and lubricant at all times. The diaphragm pump operates on pressure difference, discharging lubricant only when the spray gun is in use. This saves compressed air that would otherwise be consumed continuously.

The built-in agitator ensures a homogeneous water–lubricant mixture at all times, while the stainless steel vessel resists corrosion and the mobile trolley allows easy movement across the forging shop floor.

System Components

What’s Inside the Unit

Agitator Unit — Stainless steel impeller sweeps the maximum cross-section of the vessel, keeping graphite lubricant in continuous suspension. Mounted on a stainless steel shaft guided in a bottom bush and coupled to the gearbox shaft by SS coupling. Driven by a 0.25 HP motor through a Bonfiglioli in-line helical gearbox with Siemens starter panel. The agitator unit can be removed by removing the top cover.
Graco Diaphragm Pump — Graco 3/4″ Husky air-operated diaphragm pump delivers lubricant on demand. Pump activates only when the spray gun trigger is pressed, saving compressed air. Includes air silencer and suction hose.
Dual-Action Spray Gun — Model DJ-10-600 with separate trigger positions for air spraying and material spraying, fitted with a 600 mm aluminium straight pipe for reach.
Pressure Regulator — Aeroflex W-02 regulator (1/4″ port) with adjustable knob for air pressure setting. Operating pressure up to 2.5 kg/cm².
Stainless Steel Vessel — 200-litre vessel fabricated from 2 mm SS 202 sheet (650 mm dia. × 655 mm height). The rigid design supports the agitator and spraying unit assembly. Corrosion-resistant with drain plug for easy cleaning. Hand lid on top cover for refilling.
Mobile Trolley — Sturdy MS square-section trolley with 4″ × 2″ cast iron castor wheels (fixed + swivel) for easy movement across the forging shop floor.
Operating Guidelines

Tips for Optimum Performance

Do not start the agitator when the vessel is empty. The vessel must contain liquid up to the impeller to avoid dry running of the shaft in the locating bush
Ensure no foreign material — cotton waste, paper, polythene bags — enters the lubricant
Supply dry air to the pump and drain the FR unit frequently
Use ESPON graphite-based and/or graphiteless die lubricants for best performance
