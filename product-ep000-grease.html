EP000 Grease — Extreme Pressure Lithium Grease

Lithium-Based Extreme Pressure Grease for Centralised Lubrication

SPS offers lithium-based extreme pressure grease manufactured using superior quality base stocks and specially customised extreme pressure additives for optimum performance. EP000 Grease meets IPSS:1-09-005 and IS:7623-1993 (second revision) specifications.

EP000 Grease is the softest and most free-flowing of the EP0 / EP00 / EP000 range — suited to centralised lubrication systems with fine distributor nozzles, where a more fluid grease is needed to maintain reliable flow without choking the system.

How It Works

Stability at High & Low Operating Temperatures

Centralised lubrication systems on roller and anti-friction bearings run at operational temperatures that may exceed 120–150°C. A grease that thins out at these temperatures causes overflow, leakage, and spillage; EP000 Grease maintains its stability at high operating temperatures, keeping the system running clean.

Even where ambient temperatures drop as low as 5°C, EP000 Grease maintains its stability and viscosity — preventing the choking of centralised lubrication systems that a grease which thickens in the cold would otherwise cause. SPS also customises EP000 Grease to suit the specific requirements of a system’s lubrication nozzles and pumps, improving lubricity and reducing wastage.

Application

Method of Application

Clean the surface thoroughly before applying EP000 Grease, manually or with a grease gun. Always avoid contamination of the grease by dust and/or dirt when applying — preferably use a pneumatic pump system.

EP000 Grease is especially developed for the lubrication of closed gears, and can also be used for chassis lubrication by means of a centralised system.
