EP1 Lithium Grease — For Hot Rolling Applications

Multi-Purpose EP Grease for Hot Rolling Mill Bearings

SPS EP1 Lithium Grease is a premium-quality, multi-purpose lubricating grease specially formulated to meet the demanding requirements of the hot rolling industry. Fortified with Extreme Pressure (EP) additives, anti-wear agents, and oxidation inhibitors, this grease ensures long-lasting protection under high loads and elevated temperatures typical of hot rolling mills.

With an NLGI Grade 1 consistency, EP1 offers a softer structure than Grade 2 greases while remaining firmer than semi-fluid grades — making it well suited to centralised lubrication systems where pumpability and bearing retention must be balanced. It performs smoothly across a wide temperature range of −20°C to +140°C.

How It Works

EP Protection Across a Wide Temperature Range

Bearings in hot rolling mills face a combination of extreme mechanical loads and elevated temperatures that cause conventional greases to break down, thin out, or oxidise. EP1 Lithium Grease is formulated with superior EP additives that create a durable protective film on bearing surfaces — preventing metal-to-metal contact, seizure, and premature wear even under high-load conditions.

The lithium-based thickener system provides outstanding resistance to thermal breakdown and water washout. With a drop point of 190°C and water washout below 7% at 79°C, EP1 maintains its structure in the wet, high-temperature environments of rolling mill operations. Its NLGI Grade 1 consistency ensures reliable flow through centralised lubrication lines while providing adequate bearing retention at operating temperatures.
