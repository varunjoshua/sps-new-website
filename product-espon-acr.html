ESPON-ACR — Heat Retention & Alpha-Case Control Coating

Protective Coating for Titanium, Inconel & Critical Super Alloys

ESPON-ACR is applied to titanium, Inconel, and other critical super alloys before heating, to retain heat in the billet and substantially reduce alpha-case formation on the surface. It is best suited to finish-machined parts that see no further shaping after heat treatment — once heating is complete, the coating comes off easily by hand.

Why It Matters

Heat Loss & Alpha-Case in Titanium Forging

Heating titanium, Inconel, and similar critical super alloys demands precise temperature control. Titanium billets lose heat very quickly once out of the furnace, which makes subsequent processing such as forging difficult and can cause rejection when required properties are not met.

Titanium is also prone to forming a hard alpha-case layer on its surface during high-temperature heating — much like oxidation and decarburisation in steel. Alpha-case causes part rejection at final testing and has to be ground off, a slow process since hardened titanium does not machine easily.

Coating titanium billets with ESPON-ACR before heating helps them retain heat so that forging can proceed as planned, while substantially reducing alpha-case formation. The coating has no impact on heating time, temperature, or the properties of the part after heating.

Application

Method of Application

ESPON-ACR can be applied by brushing, spraying, or dipping.

Removal

Once heat treatment is complete, ESPON-ACR is removed by hand — making it well suited to finish-machined parts that require no further shot blasting, grinding, or machining afterward.
