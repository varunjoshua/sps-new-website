ESPON-ACR3 — Oxidation Resistant Coating

Protective Coating for Titanium, Inconel & Special Alloys

ESPON-ACR3 is a proprietary protective coating for critical alloys. It prevents high-temperature oxidation during heating for hot forming of titanium, stainless steel, Inconel, and other special alloys. It is suited to parts that undergo further shaping after heating — the coating comes off naturally during that process rather than needing to be removed by hand.

How It Works

Retaining Heat, Reducing Alpha-Case

Protecting titanium billets with ESPON-ACR3 before heating results in retention of heat in the heated billets, so that forging or further processing can be carried out as planned, while substantially reducing alpha-case formation. The coating has no impact on heating time, temperature, or properties after heating of coated parts.

This matters because during heating of titanium, Inconel, and such highly critical super alloys, much care is required to ensure that forging is done at precisely controlled temperature. In the case of titanium forging, heat is lost very fast from heated billets, thereby making subsequent processing like forging very difficult, and heat loss from titanium billets can cause rejection due to required properties not being met. Additionally, similar to oxidation and decarburisation in steels, titanium is prone to formation of alpha-case on the surface during heating at high temperatures — which causes rejection of parts during final testing and must be removed by grinding after forming the parts, a laborious process, as hard titanium is not easily machinable.

Application

Method of Application

ESPON-ACR3 is ready-to-use and can be applied by brushing or dipping.

1
After opening the container, stir the contents with a clean stirrer for 20 minutes so that the bottom sludge is completely mixed with the liquid.
2
Ensure the surface to be coated is dry, and free from grease, oil, rust, and dust. Achieve this by swabbing the surface with a piece of cloth dipped in ESPON-ACR3 — a very thin film is left, which enables adherence of the subsequent coating by brushing.
3
Use a clean brush to apply the coating. ESPON-ACR3 can also be applied by dipping.
4
Allow the coating to dry well before charging the coated billets into the heating furnace.
5
After use, close the container air-tight and store in a cool place.
