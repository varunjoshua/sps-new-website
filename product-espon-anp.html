ESPON-ANP — Anti-Nitriding Stop-Off Paste

Selective Nitriding with Anti-Nitriding Stop-Off Protection

Gas nitriding and plasma ion nitriding are case hardening processes that improve wear resistance, seizing resistance, corrosion resistance, and fatigue life of components. Selective nitriding is often necessary — only specific areas of a component need hardening while the rest must remain unaffected. ESPON-ANP is applied to areas that must be protected from nitriding during the gas nitriding process.

ESPON-ANP Anti-Nitriding Compound — HDPE container
How It Works

Why Selective Nitriding Is Necessary

ESPON-ANP is applied to surfaces where nitriding must be prevented. The paste acts as a barrier, blocking nitrogen penetration on coated areas while leaving uncoated surfaces free for case hardening. After the nitriding cycle, the paste is removed with a brass wire brush or hand tap, leaving clean, un-nitrided surfaces.

Barrel Screws for Injection Moulding

Barrel screws are nitrided to increase wear resistance. However, threaded areas become brittle if nitrided. ESPON-ANP is used to mask and protect threaded areas from nitriding.

Hot Forging Dies

Forging dies are nitrided to increase service life. However, machining fully nitrided dies during die sinking is time-consuming and wears out expensive machine tools. Only the impression area needs nitriding — the rest is coated with ESPON-ANP to allow easier machining afterwards.

Application

Method of Application

ESPON-ANP is applied using a soft brush. Two coats are required with drying time between coats.

1
Stir the contents thoroughly with a wooden plank so that the bottom sludge is completely mixed.
2
Degrease the components thoroughly. The surface must be clean and free from oil or grease.
3
Apply the first coat using a soft brush (artist’s no. 8). Ensure uniform coverage on all areas to be protected.
4
Allow to dry for 1 hour, then apply a second coat. Two coats are essential for reliable protection.
5
After drying, inspect the coating for cracks. Repair any cracks before the component enters the furnace.
6
After nitriding, remove the coating with a brass wire brush or hand tap.
