ESPON-DLO — Oil-Based Hot Forging Die Lubricant

Graphite-Based Coolant-Cum-Lubricant for Hot Forging Dies

ESPON-DLO is an excellent graphite-based coolant-cum-lubricant for hot forging dies, suited to hot forging hammers and presses. It is used directly, or diluted with low-viscosity mineral oil if found to be too thick, and applied by swabbing or spraying.

Why It Matters

Die Cost & Die Life in a Forge Shop

In a forge shop, die cost amounts to 10–15% of the total forging cost — making die life one of the biggest levers on profitability. Poor lubrication increases forging load, disrupts metal flow, and can cause the forged component to stick in the die cavity, all of which accelerate die wear and drive up cost per part.

ESPON-DLO improves die life and, in turn, automatically improves the profitability of the forge shop.

Application

Method of Application

After opening the container, stir the contents well with a clean wooden plank. It is then ready for use by swabbing or spraying.

Dilution

If the lubricant is found to be very thick, dilute it with low-viscosity mineral oil.
