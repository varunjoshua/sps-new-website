ESPON-GK3 Hot Forging Die Lubricant — 200 kg special HDPE container
How It Works

Eliminating Smoke, Extending Die Life

Conventional oil-based die lubricants burn off on contact with hot dies, generating heavy smoke and pollution on the shop floor while offering little protection to the die surface — leading to reduced die life.

ESPON-GK3’s water-miscible, graphite-based formulation eliminates this smoke entirely, in hot forging hammers and presses alike, while helping optimise die life.

Application

Method of Application

ESPON-GK3 is mixed with water in a 1:10 up to 1:30 dilution ratio, depending on forged part complexity, weight, and method of die lubrication. Different forgings call for different dilution ratios — the exact ratio for a given part is determined by a series of trials.

Recommended Equipment

Best results are obtained when ESPON-GK3 is sprayed using the SPS die spray system and DSP2 spray gun.
