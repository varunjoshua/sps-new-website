ESPON-HF — Anti-Scale Protective Coating

Control Decarburization & Reduce Scale Loss in Hot Rolling and Forging

ESPON-HF is an effective protective coating for blooms and billets that controls decarburization to a minimum level while substantially reducing scaling during heating. It improves productivity and reduces cost in hot rolling and hot forging operations.

How It Works

Preventing Decarburization & Scale Formation

During high-temperature heating for hot rolling or forging, oxygen reacts with the metal surface to form scale, while carbon migrates from the steel — a process called decarburization. Decarburization leaves the metal unfit for critical components like rails, leaf springs, and automotive parts where carbon content must be maintained within specification.

ESPON-HF is applied to billets and ingots before furnace entry. The coating forms a protective barrier that prevents atmospheric oxygen from reacting with the steel surface, controlling both scale formation and decarburization to negligible levels with no impact on heating time or temperature.

Application

Method of Application

ESPON-HF is ready to use. It can be applied by airless spraying, brushing, or dipping.

1
Stir the contents thoroughly with a clean stirrer so that the bottom sludge is completely mixed with the liquid.
2
Ensure the surface to be coated is dry and free from grease, oil, rust, and dust.
3
Using airless spraying equipment, coat the blooms or billets with ESPON-HF. Brushing or dipping may also be used.
4
Allow the coating to dry at 70–120°C. If required, apply a second coat by spraying.
