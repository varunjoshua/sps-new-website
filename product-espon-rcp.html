ESPON-RCP — Refractory Coating Compound

Zircon-Based Refractory Coating for Furnace Linings

ESPON-RCP is a zircon-based refractory coating with special additives and powerful binders, applied to the hot face of furnace linings. It withstands temperatures up to 1900°C and resists corrosive gases.

ESPON-RCP is optimally applied to new refractory linings. If used on old or partially worn-out linings, the lining must first be properly repaired — for example, using ESPON-RC3 powder — before coating.

ESPON-RCP Refractory Coating Compound
How It Works

Reflecting Heat, Preventing Thermal Shock

ESPON-RCP reflects heat back into the furnace and prevents thermal shock cracking of the refractory lining, while increasing its emissivity. This heat retention allows furnaces that are shut down overnight to stay warmer, reducing next-morning startup time by up to 1.5 hours in some cases. In sealed quench furnaces, ESPON-RCP improves heat uniformity and reduces outer shell temperature.

This matters because refractory lining wears over time from thermal shock cracking, flame impingement, corrosive combustion products, carbon buildup, and manual scraping — all of which increase the time and fuel needed to reach target temperature. A furnace with a new refractory lining may take 3.5 hours to reach 1250°C; after 8 to 10 months of lining wear, the same furnace can take 4 to 4.5 hours to reach the same temperature.

Application

Where ESPON-RCP Is Used

ESPON-RCP is used in rolling mill furnaces, billet re-heating furnaces, and sealed quench furnaces. It is optimally applied to new refractory linings.

Worn Linings

If applying to old or partially worn-out refractory lining, care must be taken to repair the lining properly — for example, using ESPON-RC3 powder — before applying ESPON-RCP.
