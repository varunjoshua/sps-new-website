Die Lubricant Spray Systems

Precision spray systems and guns engineered for correct die lubricant application — ensuring uniform die coverage, optimal die temperature control, and minimum lubricant wastage.

Water-based die lubricants are most effective when applied by spraying. SPS offers sprayguns with customised spray nozzles that ensure a uniform layer of atomised die lubricant is sprayed on the die, with controllable flow rate and air pressure — leading to zero wastage and optimum spray as per the profile of every forging die.
