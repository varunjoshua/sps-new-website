O-CLEAN3 — Industrial Cleaning Solution

Water-Based Oil Cleaning for Machinery & Shop Floors

Oil-based fuels and lubricants are essential consumables in hot forging, hot rolling, and heat treatment operations. Oils leak and dirty shop floors, machinery, and workmen’s hands. O-CLEAN3 is an environment-friendly, water-based cleaning solution that effortlessly dissolves all types of oil and grease — without acids, solvents, or flammable kerosene.

The dissolved oil and impurities are completely removed by washing with water. No scraping, repeated scrubbing, or expensive cleaning equipment required.

How It Works

Simple, Effective Cleaning

O-CLEAN3 works by dissolving oil, grease, and lubricants on contact. The dissolved oil and impurities can then be completely removed from floors and equipment by washing with water. There is no need for acids, flammable solvents, scraping, or repeated scrubbing.

For shop floors with thick oil-mud cake, apply the solution 2–3 times for a completely oil-free surface. Regular maintenance with a small quantity keeps the floor clean thereafter.

Dilution Ratio

1 litre of O-CLEAN3 concentrate is mixed with 5 to 15 litres of water, depending on the application and level of contamination.
